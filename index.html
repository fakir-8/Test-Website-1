Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from LUMINA

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Bkash, Nagad, and Rocket. For digital payments, send money to our number and enter the Transaction ID during checkout.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery within Dhaka takes 1–3 working days. Outside Dhaka typically takes 3–5 working days. You will receive a confirmation call within 24 hours of placing your order.
What is the return and exchange policy?
We accept returns within 7 days for manufacturing defects or wrong items sent. Products must be unused and in original condition. Please contact us on WhatsApp with photos of the issue.
What are the shipping charges?
Shipping within Dhaka is ৳60. Shipping outside Dhaka is ৳150.
How do I track my order?
After placing your order you will receive a confirmation SMS. Our team will call you before dispatch. You can also reach us on WhatsApp at 01700-000000 for updates on your order status.
Are your products authentic and original?
Yes. All LUMINA products are sourced and quality-checked in-house. We take pride in providing genuine, high-quality Bangladeshi fashion items.
